Research that starts with meaning.

I develop Reader-grounded NLP with my collaborators: a research direction that makes human interpretation part of the modelling target. XHS-SCoRE establishes a benchmark for reader-perceived social comparison; the cue-visibility audit examines the linguistic evidence models use to predict those responses.

Alongside this work, I study how multilingual evaluation interfaces shape measured performance. Across these projects, I connect research questions to data-collection protocols, controlled experiments and inspectable code.

I am a PhD researcher in Linguistics at Trinity College Dublin and a Government of Ireland Postgraduate Scholar. My doctoral research focuses on Tanglang, an endangered Ngwi language, and contact-induced phonological change.

Before Trinity, I worked in computational-linguistics research at the Education University of Hong Kong. My training in linguistics and psychological science at the University of Sydney continues to shape how I connect model behaviour to linguistic and human evidence.
