Agent tools & integration.

EmailHub MCP connects a self-hosted email workspace to AI assistants through authenticated Model Context Protocol tools. Go, SQLite and Bleve support bilingual search, resumable indexing and revision-controlled drafts, with account permissions and recorded send outcomes.
