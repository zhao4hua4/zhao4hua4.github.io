Privacy

Your visit.
Your choice.

Optional analytics help me understand which research and software people find useful, and where the website can be clearer.

Choose how you browse.

Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity load only after you accept analytics. Declining, closing the notice or continuing to browse leaves them off until you accept. You can change your choice at any time using Analytics settings in the footer.

Your choice is stored in this browser for 180 days. Turning analytics off stops future collection and removes this site’s Google Analytics and Clarity cookies. It does not erase data already received by the providers.

What analytics collect.

Google Analytics 4 measures page visits, traffic sources, approximate location, device and browser information, engagement and interactions with links. Google processes IP addresses to derive location and then discards them; the dashboard does not show raw visitor IP addresses. Advertising storage, Google signals and advertising personalisation are disabled in this site’s tag. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Analytics data collection.

Microsoft Clarity provides session replays and heatmaps based on page content, clicks, scrolling and other browsing interactions, alongside device and approximate location information. The Contact email disclosure is masked in recordings. Clarity’s advertising consent is denied. Microsoft also processes Clarity data under its own privacy terms, including to improve its products. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement and Clarity privacy information.

These tools describe visits and behaviour; they do not tell me a visitor’s name, employer or email address. The site does not assign custom visitor identities. Custom link events use a category and destination domain, without copying email addresses or URL query strings.

Browser storage and retention.

The hz-analytics-consent local-storage entry records only your choice, its date and the notice version. It is used to remember your preference, including a refusal. After acceptance, Google Analytics can set _ga and _ga_P082GT179E, and Clarity can set _clck and _clsk. These analytics cookies distinguish visits and sessions; their lifetimes are controlled by the providers and project settings.

Clarity’s standard session recordings are retained for 30 days; labelled or favourited recordings and heatmaps can be retained for nine months. Google Analytics retention depends on the property’s settings. See Clarity retention and Google Analytics retention.

Hosting and privacy questions.

This site is operated by Hua Zhao and hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical request information when it serves a page, independently of your analytics choice. External sites have their own privacy practices. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

For questions or requests about your data, use the Contact page. Google and Microsoft may process data internationally under the safeguards described in their privacy policies. You may also contact your local data protection authority, including Ireland’s Data Protection Commission.
