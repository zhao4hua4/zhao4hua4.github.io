NLP & model evaluation

Research concept

Reader-grounded NLP

A research direction I develop with my collaborators, bringing the reader and their experienced meaning into NLP targets, benchmarks and model diagnostics.

The same expression can prompt different responses in different readers. Reader-grounded NLP makes that relationship explicit: model the response to a text in a defined reader context, then examine the evidence behind the prediction.
