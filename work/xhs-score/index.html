Bring the reader into the prediction target.

A benchmark of reader-perceived social comparison: 13,916 Chinese social-media posts, model comparisons and human validation.

The contribution

XHS-SCoRE puts reader-grounded NLP into practice. With my collaborators, I connect a defined reader-response target to data collection, model evaluation and behavioural validation, using social-comparison direction in Chinese social-media posts.

Why it matters

Language AI increasingly operates in settings where interpretation varies across people. Reader-grounded evaluation gives research teams a way to specify whose experience a system should model, collect evidence about that experience, and diagnose where model predictions diverge.

What the evidence shows

The reader-grounded signal is learnable in-domain, while prompted inference remains fragile. The best supervised Chinese encoder reaches 0.679 Macro-F1; the primary zero-shot prompted conditions range from 0.400 to 0.518. Generated stimuli elicit measurable reader responses in the human pilot.

How it works

01

Specify the reader–text relation

Model p(y | x, r): the response y to expression x for reader context r. The benchmark distinguishes upward, downward and no-clear-comparison responses.

02

Ground labels in immediate response

A collection protocol with 67 active Xiaohongshu users yields 13,916 posts and fixed training, validation and test splits.

03

Connect evaluation and human validation

Compare supervised encoders and prompted LLMs, inspect directional errors, and examine responses to generated stimuli in a 29-participant pilot.

Research context & materials

Text-only Xiaohongshu posts, a defined reader group, and fixed in-domain splits: 8,350 training, 2,783 validation and 2,783 test posts. Supervised and zero-shot results describe their respective training settings. The 29-participant pilot evaluates generated stimuli.

Protocols, model runners, prompts, generated examples, predictions and aggregate diagnostics are available under CC BY-NC 4.0. Raw platform posts remain restricted.
